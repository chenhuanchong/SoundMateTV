⚠️ Prominent Disclosure: Android AccessibilityService API Usage

SoundMateTV uses the Android AccessibilityService API strictly to deliver core remote control enhancement functions on Android TV.

  • Remote Control Key Interception: Detects TV remote volume and shortcut button events (Volume Up/Down/Mute, D-Pad shortcuts) to adjust your Sonos speaker volume and display custom TV HUD overlays and dials.
  • Active App Profile Switching: Observes window state changes solely to retrieve the foreground media app package name, enabling per-app sound profile memory and custom HUD positioning.

Data Commitment: The Accessibility Service is strictly local. It NEVER captures, records, stores, or transmits typed keystrokes, passwords, payment info, personal messages, or on-screen content.

1. Introduction & Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the SoundMateTV application (referred to as "the App", "we", "us", or "our"), designed for Android TV and Google TV devices to interface with Sonos home audio systems.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. SoundMateTV is built from the ground up as a privacy-first, offline-capable companion tool. All discovery and control routines run entirely within your home local network.

3. Permissions Used by the Application

SoundMateTV requests only the minimal permissions required to function as an audio companion on Android TV:

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Used solely to discover and send UPnP/SOAP commands to Sonos speakers within your local Wi-Fi / LAN network. No external cloud connections are made.
  • CHANGE_WIFI_MULTICAST_STATE: Allows the app to send and receive SSDP multicast packets for automatic speaker discovery on your local network.
  • SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW: Allows the app to render floating volume HUD bars and control dials on top of other TV apps.
  • BIND_ACCESSIBILITY_SERVICE: Enables instantaneous remote control button listening and active media app identification (detailed in the Prominent Disclosure above).
  • PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS: An alternative fallback to detect active foreground media player apps for independent volume memory when accessibility service is not active.
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED: Optional permission to automatically launch background audio synchronization when the TV powers on.

4. Third-Party Services and SDKs

SoundMateTV does NOT integrate any third-party tracking, advertising, analytics, or profiling frameworks (such as Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, Facebook SDK, or AdMob).

The app communicates solely with your Sonos hardware via standard open network protocols (UPnP, SSDP, and GENA events) over your private local area network.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Our app does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13. Since the app collects no personal information whatsoever, it poses zero privacy risk to children.

6. Security of Your Data

Because all settings and speaker associations are stored strictly inside your TV's sandboxed local application storage (Android SharedPreferences) and all network requests are strictly confined to your local subnet, your data is as secure as your home Wi-Fi network.
